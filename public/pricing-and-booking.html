Starting at $50 per contractor per set, plus attendance coverage. This is an estimate, not a confirmed quote.

Attendance is rounded up to each started block of 100 people, per set. The rate is $15 per block, or $40 per block when venue capacity exceeds 400. Photography bookings above 300 expected attendees include at least two photographers.
